Choose a mark

Primary: horizontal placements. Stacked: square or portrait placements. Wordmark: name-only placement when the bird is already nearby. Emblem: badges and small icons.

Use light-bg on white/light surfaces; its MFMA TEAM subline is blue. Use dark-bg on dark surfaces; its subline is yellow. Black and white are true single-color alternatives.

Size and clear space

Allow clear space of at least one quarter of the badge diameter around the complete mark. Keep proportions fixed. Start with the primary at 320 pixels or 70 mm wide and the emblem at 48 pixels or 12 mm; inspect at actual output size. Use the emblem for tiny placements. Feather and eye details simplify at 32 pixels.

Production

SVG and PDF files are vector artwork with outlined lettering. PNG logos have alpha transparency, while the white badge stays opaque. JPGs have white backgrounds. Social files are opaque and ready to use. Colors are RGB: ask the print provider for the final profile conversion and proof. For small embroidery, use a single-color emblem and confirm detail reproduction with the fabricator.
